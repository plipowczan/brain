# Richard van der Blom

## 🗒️ Description
LinkedIn algorithm researcher and B2B social selling strategist based in Valencia. 15+ years analyzing what works on LinkedIn at scale — has trained 350,000+ professionals at 1,000+ companies including PwC, Deloitte, Capgemini, Siemens, Nestlé, UBS. Publishes the **Algorithm Insights Report** twice yearly, considered an industry-standard reference for LinkedIn content/engagement mechanics. Rated #1 Most Influential LinkedIn Creator in the Netherlands by Favikon (Feb 2024).

Relevant as an external reference for [[LinkedIn Strategy]] — his data-backed playbook complements the personal-strategy notes here.
