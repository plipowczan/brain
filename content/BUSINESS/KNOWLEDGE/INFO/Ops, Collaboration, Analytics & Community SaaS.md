# Ops, Collaboration, Analytics & Community SaaS

A collective reference for the operational, analytics, community, and region-specific SaaS used across my projects and client engagements. These are well-known products kept here as grouped entries rather than standalone notes.

**Project key:** Q = [[Qamera AI]] · AGRE = [[AGRE]] · TTTR = [[Tech To The Rescue]] · JG = [[Jakub Głąb Agent System]] · VB = [[Value Builders]] · SS = [[Agentic Skills Submodules|shared-skills]]

## 🧩 Collaboration & productivity
- **Slack** — team communication + a channel for system/pipeline notifications and Slack-fronted agents. *Used by: TTTR.* — [slack.com](https://slack.com/)
- **Google Workspace** — Docs/Drive/Sheets/Forms; shared-drive storage doubles as the knowledge-base substrate for a file-native agent. *Used by: TTTR, JG, VB.* — [workspace.google.com](https://workspace.google.com/)
- **Asana** — task/project management; exposed to an agent through a whitelisted MCP server. *Used by: JG.* — [asana.com](https://asana.com/)
- **Loom** — async video recording for briefings/walkthroughs without a live meeting. *Used by: VB.* — [loom.com](https://www.loom.com/)
- **Figma** — collaborative UI/UX design and prototyping. *Used by: TTTR, VB.* — [figma.com](https://www.figma.com/)

## 📊 Analytics & BI
- **Metabase** — open-source BI / dashboards over the application database. *Used by: TTTR.* — [metabase.com](https://www.metabase.com/)
- **Hotjar** — session replay + heatmaps for qualitative UX insight. *Used by: VB.* — [hotjar.com](https://www.hotjar.com/)
- **Microsoft Clarity** — free session analytics (replays + heatmaps); the zero-cost option taught for MVPs. *Used by: VB.* — [clarity.microsoft.com](https://clarity.microsoft.com/)

> For product analytics + flags the vault's primary is [[PostHog]]; these are lighter/complementary.

## 👥 Community & education
- **Skool** — cohort/community platform hosting a course's community + content. *Used by: VB.* — [skool.com](https://www.skool.com/)
- **Circle** — community platform (alternative to Skool) for courses/memberships. *Used by: VB.* — [circle.so](https://circle.so/)
- **Reforge** — product/growth education and frameworks. *Used by: VB.* — [reforge.com](https://www.reforge.com/)

## 🇪🇺 EU / region-specific business
- **inFakt** — Polish invoicing/accounting platform with an API; powers automated invoicing in the shared-skills CFO tooling. *Used by: SS.* — [infakt.pl](https://www.infakt.pl/)
- **Signaturit** — Spanish/EU e-signature provider (eIDAS-compliant) for contracts. *Planned: AGRE.* — [signaturit.com](https://www.signaturit.com/)
- **WhatsApp Business API** — customer messaging / chatbot channel, dominant in the Iberian market. *Planned: AGRE.* — [business.whatsapp.com](https://business.whatsapp.com/)

## 💻 Terminal
- **Warp** — modern, AI-assisted terminal (blocks, command suggestions); mentioned as a dev-environment option in training. *Used by: VB.* — [warp.dev](https://www.warp.dev/)
