# AI Voices to Follow on LinkedIn (2026)

## 🗒️ Description

A curated "who-to-follow" list from a [LinkedIn post by René Remsik](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7481270754404225024/) — **40 profiles across 8 AI niches**. Advice in the post: don't follow all 40 — pick the niche closest to your goals and start with 5.

Low-signal listicle by nature (much of it mutual promotion), captured here as a **starting map of AI creators by niche**, not an endorsement. Complements [[LinkedIn Strategy]] (how to *post*) — this is who to *read*.
