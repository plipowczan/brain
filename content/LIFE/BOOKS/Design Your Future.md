# 🚀 The Book in 3 Sentences
Brian Tracy's systematic approach to taking control of your life through deliberate goal-setting and long-term planning. The premise: the future is not something that happens to you but something you design — by clarifying values, setting written goals, and reverse-engineering the daily actions that get you there. Sits in the same goal-focus lineage as [[The One Thing]] and the deliberate-spending philosophy of [[Die with Zero]].
