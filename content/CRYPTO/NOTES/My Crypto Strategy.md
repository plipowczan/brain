# My Crypto Strategy

## 🗒️ Description

Crypto is a **large part of my portfolio** — the high-volatility, convex engine of my overall plan ([[My Investment Strategy]]). This note is the dedicated playbook for that sleeve: *how* I invest in it, not whether I should.

The strategy is **cycle-based**. It rests on Bitcoin's repeating ~4-year [[Bitcoin Cyclicality|halving cycle]] and a discipline very close to **Phil Konieczny's**: accumulate patiently through the bear, hold through the bull, and — the part I failed at last time — **take profit into euphoria**. It is built deliberately as the antidote to the specific mistakes I made in 2021–22 ([[What mistakes I made on the crypto market in 2021-2022]]).

> One sentence: **BTC and ETH only, accumulate in the cold, sell into the heat, and never bet on something I can't explain.**

**November 2026 update.** I left crypto about a year ago and hold much of that money in stablecoins. On 7–8 Nov 2026 I wrote the re-entry down: target weights, a 6-month accumulation plan with a crash reserve, a no-buy list, the skeleton of the take-profit ladder with a hard ceiling, and custody rules after a look at what my platforms actually guarantee. The sections below carry it; amounts stay in my private notes.

## 🧩 What I Learned the Hard Way (2021–22)

The whole strategy is a reaction to documented errors (full list: [[What mistakes I made on the crypto market in 2021-2022]]):

- **Didn't take profit** → the cardinal sin. A 4-year cycle gives back most paper gains if you don't sell. *Fix: a written take-profit ladder.*
- **FOMO & chased influencer calls** → bought late, on emotion. *Fix: DCA on a schedule, size from a plan, ignore the hype phase.*
- **Owned things I didn't understand** (Luna, late parachains, random projects) → *Fix: BTC and ETH only.* If I can't explain the asset in two sentences, I don't own it ([[Cognitive Biases in Investing]] — familiarity vs real understanding).
- **What actually worked:** diversifying custody and moving BTC to a **Ledger**. Keep doing that.

## 🧩 The Cycle I Trade

Bitcoin's halving (~every 4 years) drives a repeatable four-phase rhythm — mechanics, dates, and historical stats are in [[Bitcoin Cyclicality]]. My behavior per phase:

| Phase | What it feels like | What I do |
|-------|--------------------|-----------|
| **Accumulation** (post-crash, despair) | Nobody cares, price flat & low | **DCA hardest here.** Best risk/reward. Buy BTC/ETH mechanically. |
| **Markup** (bull, hope→belief) | Price rising, news turning good | Hold. Keep base DCA. Do *not* add leverage or chase. |
| **Distribution / Euphoria** (mania) | "This time is different", everyone's a genius | **Execute the take-profit ladder.** Sell into strength, in tranches. This is the phase I failed last cycle. |
| **Markdown** (bear, denial→capitulation) | −70/−80% drawdowns, "crypto is dead" | Don't panic-sell. Hold the core, redeploy taken profit back into accumulation. Loop restarts. |

> The phases are reliable; the **timing is not**. Indicators (MVRV-Z, Pi Cycle, 200-week MA, Fear & Greed) are a *compass for which phase I'm in*, never a clock — same discipline as the equities [[Bear Markets — 100 Years of History#🧩 Part IV — Indicators — a Compass, Not a Clock|compass]]. I never try to nail the exact top.

## 🧩 The Rules — What I Do

1. **BTC and ETH only.** The two assets I actually understand. Everything else is a pass, however good the story. **Two written exceptions (Nov 2026):** CRO, held for the exchange card's perks (the new lock-up terms are under review), and NEXO, held only until its exit (the first ladder rung, or earlier on a warning sign). No others.
2. **DCA through the bear and accumulation phase.** Fixed amount, fixed schedule, emotion-free — the opposite of my FOMO buying last cycle. **Nov 2026 plan:** a fixed weekly buy for 6 months, plus a reserve deployed at BTC **−20% and −35%** from the plan's start price (half at each). Reserve I don't spend stays cash — I don't chase a rising price with it. Stablecoins are swapped for BTC in self-custody (crypto-to-crypto, no tax event in Poland).
3. **Take profit by a written ladder** (below), into the distribution/euphoria phase. The rule sells, not my conviction.
4. **Size the sleeve so a −80% drawdown is survivable.** Crypto *will* draw down 70–85% in a bear ([[Bitcoin Cyclicality]]) — that has to be a bad year, not a wiped-out year. Position size comes from [[My Investment Strategy]]: **25% of the portfolio by default, up to 50%** in accumulation and early markup, back to 25% via the ladder in euphoria, at most 25% in a bear. Counted **without my long-term BTC core**, which is never sold (a retirement policy); stablecoins count as cash, not crypto. The 50% ceiling fails my own −50% line in the stress test (see [[My Investment Strategy]]) and I keep it knowingly.
5. **Cold storage for the core.** Long-term holdings on a hardware wallet (Ledger), not left on exchanges.
6. **Buy market-wide crashes, not broken assets.** A COVID- or war-style selloff is a reserve trigger. A bug or attack in the BTC/ETH protocol, or a ban in the US or EU, is not — then I don't buy. If a platform fails, I withdraw first and buy only from self-custody.
7. **Treat taken profit as portfolio cash**, not "house money" to gamble ([[Cognitive Biases in Investing]] — house-money effect). It either funds the next accumulation or leaves crypto entirely.

## 🧩 The Anti-Rules — What I Never Do

- **Never round-trip a full cycle without taking profit.** The 2021–22 lesson, in one line.
- **Never buy alts I can't explain.** No Luna, no "next 100x", no late-cycle narratives.
- **Never use leverage.** Liquidation turns a survivable drawdown into a zero.
- **Never chase a green candle or an influencer call.** Schedule and plan decide entries, not FOMO.
- **Never try to time the precise top or bottom.** I scale out and scale in over ranges, in tranches.
- **Never confuse a bull market for skill** ([[Investing Psychology]] — overconfidence). The cycle did the work.

## 🧩 Take-Profit Plan

The single most important fix. A pre-committed ladder so I sell *into* strength mechanically, while it's emotionally hardest:

- Scale **out** in tranches across the distribution phase (e.g. sell a fixed % of the position at successive targets / on successive indicator signals), rather than one all-or-nothing exit.
- Bias toward keeping a **BTC core** through the cycle (Phil Konieczny's "return to Bitcoin"), trimming the rest.
- Decide *in calm* what % I'm willing to ride down, and route taken profit to cash/ballast in [[My Investment Strategy]].
- **Ladder skeleton (Nov 2026):** 4–5 rungs. A rung fires only when price **and** at least one cycle indicator agree — confluence, not a single gauge ([[Bitcoin Cyclicality]], Part V). The last rung brings crypto back to 25% of the portfolio. Proceeds go to the non-crypto part of the portfolio, not back into the next pump.
- **Backstop:** several classic top indicators (Pi Cycle, MVRV thresholds) misfired in 2025. So crypto above **50%** of the portfolio (without the BTC core) triggers a trim back to the ceiling even when the cycle indicators stay silent.
- **NEXO** goes at the first rung — earlier if a warning sign appears.

## 🧩 Security & Custody

- Long-term BTC/ETH on a **hardware wallet (Ledger)** — keys offline.
- Spread custody (the one thing I got right in 2021–22): not everything on a single exchange.
- Exchange only for active buying/selling; sweep to cold storage after. Exchanges and apps hold only what I'm buying or swapping in the next few weeks.
- **CeFi yield platform (Nov 2026):** it stays for the interest, but with no new deposits and no new locks, and part of the balance moves to self-custody. No deposit-guarantee scheme covers it — the "up to 100k EUR" protection is for banks, not crypto platforms. I leave at once on any warning sign: withdrawals delayed more than 2 days, trouble at the custodian or action by a regulator, an abnormal jump in the offered rate. Otherwise an annual review.
