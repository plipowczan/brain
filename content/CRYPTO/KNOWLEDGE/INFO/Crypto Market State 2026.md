# Crypto Market State 2026

## 🗒️ Description
Overview of the cryptocurrency market in 2026 — what has changed since the 2022 bear market.

## 🧩 Key changes since 2022:
- **Bitcoin ETF** — approved in the US (January 2024), traded on major exchanges. BlackRock, Fidelity, and other traditional players entered the market.
- **Bitcoin halving** — April 2024, block reward dropped to 3.125 BTC
- **Regulation** — MiCA in Europe, growing regulatory clarity in the US
- **DeFi maturation** — less speculation, more real-world use cases
- **AI + Crypto** — growing intersection (AI agents managing wallets, on-chain AI)
- **NFT hype is over** — the NFT market has shrunk significantly, only utility-focused projects remain
- **Luna/FTX aftermath** — tightened regulation, centralised exchanges under greater scrutiny
