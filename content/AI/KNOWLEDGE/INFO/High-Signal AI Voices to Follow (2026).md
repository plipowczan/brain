# High-Signal AI Voices to Follow (2026)

## 🗒️ Description

A **ship-proven** who-to-follow list for building AI/agentic systems and a second brain. Every name is anchored to a **concrete artifact** (repo, paper, essay, tool) — not follower counts. The evidence-based successor to a generic "40 LinkedIn profiles" listicle.

Two sourcing passes feed it:
- **DR** — surfaced by a deep-research fan-out and put through **3-vote adversarial verification** (24/25 claims confirmed, 1 refuted).
- **LI** — a René Remsik "40 to follow" LinkedIn listicle, **hand-vetted profile-by-profile** via browser automation (2026-07-12). 40 profiles scored 1–5 on ship-proven-substance vs promo; **15 survived (11 keep + 4 situational), 25 cut** (~62% were content-marketing / AI-video creators).

Grouped into the domains this vault works in — see [[Agentic Coding]], [[Context Engineering]], [[Building an AI Second Brain]].

### Legend
- **Src** — `DR` = deep-research-verified · `LI` = LinkedIn hand-vetted.
- 🟡 = **situational** (real substance but noisier/promo-heavy — follow only if the niche fits).
- Star/subscriber counts are **lower bounds** (already stale at capture).
