🚨 THE "PIRATE BAY" FOR OPEN LLMS IS HERE

Hugging Bay is a new verified artifact registry for downloading model weights via torrents and hosted mirrors.

But it’s far more than just a file dump.

It’s a beautifully structured catalog where every artifact is checked for provenance and license clarity before indexing.

You can easily filter across categories: → LLMs & Embeddings (EMB) → Visual (VIS) & Audio (AUD) models → Open-source Agents & Datasets → Apps, Tools, and Evals

.. and there are many more feats to explore.

They’ve also built in semantic rerank.

It means you can search for natural-language queries like "best small commercial embedding model for RAG" and actually get a clean result.

really cool stuff.

here's the website → [https://huggingbay.xyz](https://huggingbay.xyz/)

\------ ADDENDUM: I've received a lot of questions in the comments about the difference between Hugging Face and Hugging Bay

Hugging Face is the broader ecosystem for discovering, hosting, and collaborating around AI models, datasets, and apps . Hugging Bay is more focused: a verified registry for open model artifacts.

Think of it less as another model hub, and more as a structured, searchable distribution layer for open AI weights :)
