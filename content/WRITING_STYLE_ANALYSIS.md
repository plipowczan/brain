# Writing Style Analysis — Knowledge Base for the AI Agent

## Writing Style Summary

### General characteristics

**Language and tone:**

- **English only** (technical terms in English by default; this is the canonical writing language as of 2026-05-17 — see CLAUDE.md)
- Direct, practical, personal style
- Reflective, experience-driven
- First person frequently ("I", "my")
- Prefers concrete examples and practical guidance over abstract theory

**Document structure:**

- Every document begins with YAML frontmatter containing metadata:
  - `title`, `date`, `enableToc`, `openToc`, `tags`, `type`
- Hierarchical heading structure (H1, H2, H3)
- Heavy use of numbered and bulleted lists
- "Resources" section at the end with external links

**Characteristic elements:**

- Obsidian internal links in the format `[[path/to/file]]`
- Emoji in section headings (🚀, 🎨, ☘️, ✍️, 📒, 🗒️, 🔗, 🧩, 📖)
- TODO comments in the format `#todo` or `#todo/replace`
- Dataview queries for dynamic content rendering
- Book quotes are often emphasized with bold

**Content organization:**

- Often opens with a general introduction or framing context
- Topical sections with clear headings
- Bulleted lists preferred for scannability
- Closes with practical guidance or a summary

### Fragment 4: Book notes — template structure

**Source:** `LIFE/BOOKS/How to take smart notes.md`

```
# 🚀 The Book in 3 Sentences
The book is about how you should take notes in order to take advantage of them in the efficient way. It describes the slip-box technique and describe who invented it and how it worked for him. Approach to note-taking was inspired by the 20th-century German sociologist Niklas Luhmann.

# ☘️ How the Book Changed Me

This was the "why" for taking notes while reading and looking for the purpose of reading and learning specified stuff. The important thing for me was finding the purpose of note taking - it is not just collecting information; it is seeking to develop ideas and arguments. I try to read with a pen in my hand - I always did but now I know that it makes sense.

# Summary + Notes

Systematically make three types of notes to develop your ideas and arguments:
1. Fleeting notes - they don't go in a slip-box; they're simply for capturing ideas and thoughts. You can take them in a notebook, on a scrap of paper, or wherever works for you. Keep these notes in one place so you can go through them later.
2. Literature notes - notes about what you read. While you're reading, write down things you don't want to forget along with reference details. Your notes should be short and in your own words. These go into your reference slip-box– the first of the two slip-boxes.
3. Permanent notes - These will arise from your fleeting and literature notes, which you should go through regularly– preferably daily. As you review your notes, ask yourself what's relevant to your own ideas, your research, and your interests.
```

**Characteristics:**

- Template structure for book notes
- Emoji in section headings
- Personal reflections on impact
- Practical summary with bulleted lists

## Patterns and Conventions

### 1. YAML frontmatter

Every document opens with metadata:

```yaml
---
title: "Document title"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
enableToc: true
openToc: true
tags: ["tag1", "tag2"]
type: basic-note | book-note | knowledge-note
---
```

### 2. Internal links

Format: `[[path/to/file|Display text]]` or `[[path/to/file]]`

### 3. Emoji in headings

- 🚀 — The Book in 3 Sentences / main message
- 🎨 — Impressions
- ☘️ — How the Book Changed Me / impact
- ✍️ — My Top 3 Quotes
- 📒 — Summary + Notes
- 🗒️ — Description
- 🔗 — Links
- 🧩 — Features
- 📖 — Further reading

### 4. Book note structure

```
# Book title
Date Finished: date
Author: author
Tags: tags

# 🚀 The Book in 3 Sentences
# 🎨 Impressions
## How I Discovered It
## Who Should Read It?
# ☘️ How the Book Changed Me
# ✍️ My Top 3 Quotes
# 📒 Summary + Notes
# Resources
```

### 5. Knowledge note structure

```
# Title
## 🗒️ Description
## 🔗 Links
## 🧩Features:
## 📖Further reading
```

### 6. TODO comments

- `#todo` — general task to be done
- `#todo/replace` — placeholder to be replaced
- `#inbox/todo` — task sitting in the inbox

### 7. Dataview queries

Used for dynamic content rendering:

````markdown
```dataview
table title
from "CATEGORY"
```
````

## Recommendations for the AI agent

### When writing new documents

1. **Always use YAML frontmatter** with the right metadata
2. **Use emoji in section headings** per the convention
3. **Create internal links** to related documents
4. **Write in first person** for personal reflections
5. **Use bulleted lists** for scannability
6. **Add a Resources section** with external links
7. **Be concrete and practical** — avoid empty generalities
8. **Write in English** — this is the canonical vault language as of 2026-05-17
9. **Use hierarchical heading structure**
10. **Add personal reflections** and experience where it adds signal

### When editing existing documents

1. **Preserve the existing structure** and style
2. **Don't change frontmatter** without a reason
3. **Preserve internal links** and emoji
4. **Add new sections** following existing conventions
5. **Update dates** when the document is materially modified
