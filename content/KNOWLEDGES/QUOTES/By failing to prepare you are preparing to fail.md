> By failing to prepare you are preparing to fail
\- Benjamin Franklin

## ✍️ Attribution

Commonly attributed to **Benjamin Franklin**, though the exact wording does not appear verbatim in his writings; it is likely a paraphrase of sentiments he expressed about diligence and foresight.

## 📒 Meaning

Neglecting preparation is itself a form of action — one that makes failure the default outcome. The quote is a reminder that success rarely happens by accident; it is the product of deliberate planning done in advance.
