> It always takes longer than you expect, even when you take into account Hofstadter's Law
\- Douglas Hofstadter

## ✍️ Attribution

Coined by **Douglas Hofstadter** in his 1979 book *Gödel, Escher, Bach: An Eternal Golden Braid*.

## 📒 Meaning

A self-referential observation about the persistent tendency to underestimate how long tasks take. Even after you adjust your estimate knowing the law applies, the adjusted estimate will still be too optimistic — the bias is recursive and inescapable.
